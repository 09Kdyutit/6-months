dear Vanika

I need you to know what six months with you has done to me.

Before you, love was something I could explain like an idea. With you, it became a place. It became your name on my phone. It became waiting for your messages. It became smiling at pictures I already saw ten times. It became missing you in the middle of normal days because normal days feel better when you are in them.

I love your pretty face, obviously. I love your eyes, your smile, your dramatic little moods, your softness, your laugh, your voice when you are sleepy, and the way you somehow make me want to be romantic even when I am trying to act normal.

But I also love the deeper things. I love how much you care. I love how real you are with me. I love that you make me feel wanted. I love that even when we are far, you still feel close enough to affect my whole day. I love that you are not just someone I like being with. You are someone I am building my future around.

Six months is not small to me. It is six months of choosing you, missing you, learning you, laughing with you, getting annoyed and still wanting you, imagining us, and realizing that my life feels more mine when you are part of it.

Happy six months, baby. I love you, seriously. — Dyutit
